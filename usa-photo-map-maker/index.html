Frequently Asked Questions

Is the photo map maker really free?

Yes! Creating and downloading your USA photo map is completely free. We offer optional canvas printing through a partner if you want a physical print.

Do I need to create an account?

No account required. Just start adding photos. Your progress saves automatically in your browser so you can come back and finish later.

What image size should I use?

Any size works! We recommend photos at least 500px wide for best quality. The tool lets you zoom and position each photo perfectly within the state.

Can I edit a photo after adding it?

Yes! Click on any state with a photo and use the Edit button to reposition it, or Clear to remove it and start fresh.

How do I print my map?

Download your completed map, then use the "Order as Canvas Print" button to upload it to our printing partner. You can also print the PNG yourself at any local print shop.
